# Getting help with Beltr

- **FAQ:** https://beltr.app/faq (setup, formats, GPU, Unraid, pricing, refunds)
- **Support email:** support@beltr.app (license keys, order numbers, support bundles; typically answered within 24 hours)
- **Discord:** https://discord.gg/PYU9g6GsRq
- **Bug reports:** https://github.com/CasaVargas/beltr-releases/issues
- **Feature requests and questions:** https://github.com/CasaVargas/beltr-releases/discussions
- **Self-hosting guide:** https://github.com/CasaVargas/beltr-unraid
- **Refunds:** 14-day money-back guarantee, https://beltr.app/refund

A support bundle (in the app: Settings → About → Get Support Bundle) contains your logs. Email it rather than posting it publicly.
